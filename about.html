About PSL

The Pakistan Super League (PSL) is Pakistan's professional Twenty20 franchise cricket league. Franchises build their squads through player drafts, retentions, direct signings and auctions.

About this site

This is an unofficial, fan-made mock auction for entertainment. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Pakistan Super League, the Pakistan Cricket Board or any franchise. Sample players are demo data and results here are not official.

How the mock auction works

  • Each of the eight teams starts with a purse of Rs. 50 crore.
  • The auctioneer puts one player up at a time; teams bid from their private links.
  • Bids must beat the current bid by the minimum raise, and teams cannot exceed their purse or squad rules.
  • When the auctioneer confirms SOLD, the player joins the winning team's squad and everyone sees the announcement.
